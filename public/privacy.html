Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Realm of Kingdom ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Realm of Kingdom game application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name through our authentication provider (Supabase Auth).
  • Game Data: Your in-game progress, including kingdom state, resources, buildings, army composition, quest progress, and ranking scores are stored on our servers to enable cloud saves and cross-device play.
  • Analytics Data: We use Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymized usage data such as session duration, screens visited, game events (buildings built, battles fought), and general device/browser information. This data is used solely to improve the game experience.
  • Device Information: We may collect general device information such as browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and language preference.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Service
  • To save and sync your game progress across devices
  • To display global leaderboards and rankings
  • To improve game balance and user experience through analytics
  • To communicate important service updates
  • To detect and prevent cheating or abuse

3. Data Storage and Security

Your game data is stored securely using Supabase, which employs industry-standard encryption and security practices. We use HTTPS for all data transmission. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Authentication and database services (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Analytics: Anonymized usage analytics (Privacy Policy)
  • Vercel: Web hosting (Privacy Policy)

5. Advertising

Our Service may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks. These networks may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your general interests. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings or the ad network's opt-out mechanisms.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete such information.

7. Your Data Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data and account
  • Export your game data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking

8. Data Retention

We retain your game data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days. Anonymized analytics data may be retained for up to 26 months.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this privacy policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
